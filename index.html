With 2 pages on, Scroll mode lays pages out in rows and wide spreads fill a full row. Your view choices (reading mode, fit, zoom, two-page, direction, theme, previews and these settings) are saved automatically and used every time you open a book.

A backup holds your settings, reading progress, bookmarks, custom covers, reading stats and online library links. Importing merges it into this browser and keeps the most recent progress.

Reading stats

Most read

Stats stay on this device. Time counts only while a book is open and you’re active; a page counts once you stay on it for a couple of seconds.

Bookmarks

Connect an online library

Paste a public link to a folder. Tenjin reads the folder structure and lists every comic inside it.

Works with Google Drive folders (needs a free API key), web folders with a file listing, and manifest files (a JSON list of links). The README explains each one. Your link is stored only in this browser.

What’s new in Tenjin 1.2.1

  • Reading stats. See time read, pages, finished books, streaks, a daily goal ring and weekly, monthly and yearly charts. Open them from the library, or press Shift+S.
  • Better covers. Tenjin now uses the front cover a comic names in its metadata, and you can pick any page as a book’s cover from the bookmarks sheet.
